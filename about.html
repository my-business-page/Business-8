THE IDEA

Think Design,

Stylo Mylo Interior is a Singapore interior design and renovation studio established in 2019. The studio's approach starts with the individual: how you live, what you value and the atmosphere you want to come home to.

Rather than forcing every project into the same aesthetic, the aim is to develop a distinctive spatial identity and carry it consistently through planning, materials, furniture and details.
